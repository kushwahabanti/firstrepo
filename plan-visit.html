Plan Your Visit

Everything you need to know before exploring Rohtas District.

Getting There

How to Reach

The district headquarters Sasaram is well-connected by rail and road.

🚂

By Train

Sasaram Junction (SSM) is a major stop on the Delhi–Kolkata main line (Grand Chord). Direct trains from:

  • New Delhi — ~10 hours
  • Kolkata — ~7 hours
  • Patna — ~3 hours
  • Varanasi — ~2 hours
🚗

By Road

National Highway NH-2 (Grand Trunk Road) passes through Sasaram. Well-connected by state buses and private vehicles.

  • Patna — ~150 km (3 hrs)
  • Varanasi — ~130 km (2.5 hrs)
  • Gaya — ~100 km (2 hrs)
  • Dehri-on-Sone — ~25 km
✈️

By Air

The nearest airports are:

  • Varanasi (Lal Bahadur Shastri Airport) — ~130 km
  • Patna (Jay Prakash Narayan Airport) — ~150 km
  • Gaya (Bodhgaya Airport) — ~100 km
When to Go

Best Seasons

Each season brings a different charm to Rohtas.

🌤️

Winter (Oct – Feb)

⭐ Best time to visit. Pleasant weather (10–25°C), ideal for forts, trekking, and sightseeing.

🌧️

Monsoon (Jul – Sep)

Waterfalls are at their peak glory. Lush green landscapes. Roads may be muddy near hills.

☀️

Summer (Mar – Jun)

Hot (35–45°C). Visit early mornings or late evenings. Carry water and sun protection.

Be Prepared

Travel Tips

🎒

What to Carry

  • Comfortable trekking shoes for fort visits
  • Sunscreen and hat for summer trips
  • Water bottles — many sites lack shops
  • Camera — the views are incredible
  • Light raincoat during monsoon season
🍲

Local Food to Try

  • Litti Chokha — iconic Bihari delicacy
  • Sattu Paratha — protein-rich flatbread
  • Thekua — sweet wheat snack
  • Dal Pitha — steamed rice dumplings
  • Fresh sugarcane juice from roadside stalls
⚠️

Safety & Etiquette

  • Respect temple customs — remove shoes, cover head
  • Don't litter at waterfalls and natural sites
  • Travel in groups to remote hill areas
  • Carry cash — UPI works but ATMs are sparse
  • Inform locals before trekking to unfamiliar spots
