Why Visit Rohtas?

Rohtas is not just a district — it's a living museum. The land that witnessed the rise of Sher Shah Suri, the builder of the Grand Trunk Road, is home to some of India's most underrated historical and natural treasures.

Whether you're a history enthusiast, an adventure seeker, or a pilgrim — Rohtas has something extraordinary waiting for you.
